## Included hardware and tool

| Item | Quantity |
| :--- | ---: |
| M3×12 socket head screws | 8 |
| M3×20 socket head screws | 6 |
| Self-adhesive silicone pads, Ø 8 mm | 8 |

Use a 2.5 mm hex key (90×50 mm). Tighten all screws hand-tight.
